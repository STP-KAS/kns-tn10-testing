## Name rules used
- Charset a-z and 0-9. A hyphen is allowed only at interior positions, and never as `--`. This is the kns-spec envelope regex `^[a-z0-9]([a-z0-9-]*[a-z0-9])?$`. Names never start with `stp-`.
- The TN10 indexer itself is more lenient: it accepts leading or trailing hyphens and allows `_` only at the start. We use the stricter set.
- Length is uniform in 3..10 from a seeded RNG (seed `r1-2026-09-24`, xmur3+sfc32). See `scripts/gen-r1-names.mjs`.
- All 5,000 names are unique, and each was checked `available && !isReservedDomain` on the TN10 indexer. The indexer rejected 5 names and 6 local collisions occurred; all were regenerated with the same length.
- Mapping is in `r1-names.csv` (index,address,name,length,fee). Replacement names are appended to `r1-name-overrides.jsonl` whenever a name was taken before its turn (same length, seeded per wallet).

## Files / scripts
- `scripts/gen-snapshot-wallets.mjs --from 20700 --to 25699`: appended wallets. Backups are `*.bak-r1-2026-09-24T22-38-23+0200` (addresses.csv, wallets.jsonl, meta.json, privkeys jsonl).
- `scripts/treasury-fund-r1.mjs`: one funding pass, in index order. The floor is 20,000 TKAS plus the reserve the tn10-million-test still needs, plus a 100 TKAS fee reserve. The treasury key is loaded in-process only.
- `scripts/r1-fund-loop.mjs`: background loop. It runs a pass every 10 min and waits while any other treasury spender is alive. It writes `desk-fund-r1.jsonl`, `desk-fund-r1-status.json`, `desk-fund-r1.log` and `desk-fund-r1-loop-state.json`.
- `scripts/kns-snap-r1.mjs`: worker. It claims funded wallets dynamically and disjointly via `r1-claims/`, marks results in `r1-done/` and `r1-failed/`, and logs to `r1-slot<N>.jsonl` and `r1-status-slot<N>.json`.
- `scripts/r1-supervisor.mjs`: starts at 10 workers and auto-scales to 15 (`r1-max-workers`) when healthy. It backs off when the error rate goes above 5% or on 429s/timeouts, and launches only when MemAvailable is at least 1200 MB. State is in `r1-supervisor-state.json`.
- `scripts/r1-progress.sh`: progress summary.
- **Stop file:** `snapshot-wallets/r1-STOP`. It stops new launches, workers exit between wallets, and funding pauses. `r1-FUND-STOP` pauses funding only.
